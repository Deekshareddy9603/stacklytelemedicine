Our Story

From Medical Students
to a Purpose-Driven Team

Stackly Telemedicine started with a simple idea — what if quality healthcare could be just a click away? As a group of medical students, we experienced the challenges of accessing healthcare, long waiting hours and limited guidance. This inspired us to build a platform that connects people with trusted doctors, anytime, anywhere.
